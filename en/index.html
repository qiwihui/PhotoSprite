The final say is yours

From a request to an album in three steps.

01 / Ask

Describe what you want

Type, use voice input, or talk back and forth in conversation mode. Resume an earlier chat anytime.

02 / Review

See photos and proposal cards

The assistant shows the photos it found and proposes an album or cleanup. Open a photo for a closer look.

03 / Decide

Confirm before anything changes

Adjust what to keep, then choose whether to create the album or delete. iOS also confirms deletions.

Beyond the AI assistant

Open the app to find cleanup suggestions waiting.

Photos are analyzed on your iPhone. Similar shots, old and QR code screenshots, blurry photos and large videos are grouped for review, alongside monthly picks and trip album suggestions.

  • Keep the better shotSimilar photos get a recommended keeper based on quality and portrait capture.
  • Look before you cleanReview each photo and deselect what you want to keep. Deleted photos remain recoverable in Recently Deleted.
  • Only new photos after the first scanOnce indexed, new or changed photos are processed as they arrive.
Privacy & choice

Your iPhone understands the photos first.

Quality, content, text and QR code analysis runs on device. You choose whether to use the AI assistant and which provider to connect with your own API key or account.
