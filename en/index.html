On-device suggestions

Cleanup ideas the moment you open it

Your library is analyzed photo by photo on your iPhone the first time, then only new photos. Suggestions are grouped by kind; tap one to see the photos.

  • Similar photos: the best of each group recommended to keep — for people, eyes open and a clear face
  • QR code screenshots: used tickets, boarding passes and payment codes, cleared after a week
  • Old screenshots: older than 30 days, searchable by the text in them
  • Blurry or bad shots, and large videos
  • Monthly best and trip albums, picked for you
Review first

You see everything before it’s deleted

Every suggestion lists the photos first: tap to view full screen, swipe between them, play videos. Deselect anything you want to keep.

  • Every unselected photo is marked Keep; the recommended one has a ★
  • iOS confirms once more, and deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days
  • Photos you keep are never suggested for deletion again
AI assistant (optional)

Albums in a sentence

“Where did I travel this year? Make an album of the 10 best photos from each place.” “I took a screenshot of a Wi-Fi password, find it for me.” The assistant finds photos using what was analyzed on your device and turns albums and deletions into cards for you to confirm.

  • Voice input, replies read aloud, and conversation mode for back-and-forth
  • Your own API key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek), or sign in with a ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot or other subscription, or an OpenRouter account
  • An inexpensive small model by default, with the cost of every request shown; OpenRouter’s free models work too
  • Pick up any earlier chat from the history

Privacy first

Your photos are analyzed on your iPhone and the results stay there. We run no servers and don’t collect or sell any data. Only when you choose to use the assistant are your messages and photo statistics sent, with your own API key or account, directly to the AI provider you chose.
